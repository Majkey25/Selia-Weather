Scope: repository evidence, official guidance and static-page checks. This is not a legal opinion, global compliance certificate or complete WCAG audit.

- **Identity:** Matěj Teplý is the public name associated with [Majkey25](https://github.com/Majkey25); `majkeylab@gmail.com` was supplied as the project contact. An employer mentioned in a personal profile is not evidence that the employer operates this app.
- **Release monetisation:** `app/build.gradle.kts` sets payments/monetisation false and declares Ads, UMP and Billing as compile-only/debug dependencies. The existing release lint model dated 6 September contains zero such dependencies in its `package` graph. This is source/build-model evidence; recheck the final APK/AAB before distribution.
- **Privacy:** `HistoryRepository`, `HistoryShareFile`, `HistoryShare`, `WeatherRefreshJob`, WebView settings and backup rules were inspected. The policy covers local retention, the full five-year CSV, question/period context, the app confirmation followed by Android recipient selection, and optional background refresh of saved coordinates. It does not imply the AI-sharing confirmation controls earlier NASA archive requests.
- **Maps:** `radar-forecast.js` requests a 7×7 coordinate grid from Open-Meteo. Observed radar and sampled model forecasts are distinguished. The disclosure now includes that additional coordinate transfer.
- **Review fixes by the main agent:** the full Leaflet BSD notice is now present at `app/src/main/assets/leaflet-LICENSE.txt` and documented in `NOTICE.md`. `RadarScreen.kt` now appends the Selia Weather identity to its User-Agent, matching the point picker.
- **Web pages:** privacy, terms, refund and cookie pages share local CSS and a local decorative brand image with adjacent text. No scripts, forms, external fonts or embedded third-party content were added. `PRIVACY.md` points to the canonical page instead of duplicating it.
- **Static accessibility checks:** four pages passed HTML nesting, one-h1, language, main landmark, skip-target, image-alt presence, current-navigation and local-link/asset checks. Text/link/date colours have measured contrast of at least 9.90:1 against the page background; the focus colour is 14.51:1. Zoom is not restricted. These results do not prove rendered layout or assistive-technology behaviour.
- **Publication build:** the existing `run_static_feed` CLI built the site in documentation mode, with a diagnostic manifest and zero forecast tiles. This did not deploy or enable calibration.
- **Existing public hosting:** a HEAD request to the current HTTPS privacy URL returned 200 without `Set-Cookie` on 8 September. That single response does not guarantee that the hosting provider never changes its behaviour.

## Remaining checks and boundaries

1. **Trader/registration details:** no app-operator postal address, business registration, VAT status or trader classification was established in this pass. None was invented. Determine whether additional Czech/EU trader disclosures apply before treating the commercial checklist as complete. Free pricing alone does not settle legal status.
2. **Controller roles and transfers:** the pages disclose purposes, legal bases, provider recipients and international processing. The actual provider/controller relationships, transfer safeguards and support-mail retention process still require an operational/legal review. No DPA, SCC or adequacy coverage is claimed without evidence. Implement the published support-retention criteria in actual mailbox handling.
3. **Distribution notices:** project MIT licensing does not replace dependency/data licences. Verify the added Leaflet notice and Android dependency notices in the final distribution. The icon was supplied for use by the user; underlying third-party rights were not independently established here.
4. **Provider access:** free Open-Meteo access is limited to non-commercial use and weighted quotas. Optional support does not establish commercial-use permission. Confirm provider eligibility before ads, subscriptions, paid features or other monetisation. OSM requires visible attribution, caching and an identifying app User-Agent or valid platform identification. Both map WebViews now explicitly identify the app; final outgoing-header/cache behaviour remains a runtime check.
5. **Browser/assistive QA:** the available Brave browser tool rejected the local `file://` page under its URL security policy. No alternative route was used to bypass that rejection. Keyboard-only navigation, 200–400% zoom, narrow-screen rendering, screen-reader output and public HTTPS delivery of the new pages remain runtime acceptance checks after deployment.
