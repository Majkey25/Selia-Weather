# Beta.18 verification

- Android: 319 unit tests, debug/release lint, APKs and signed AAB passed locally.
- JavaScript: 24 radar/point-picker tests passed. Research: 308 tests, Ruff and strict Pyright passed.
- PR #13 Android and research CI passed before merge.
- Huawei YAL-L21, Android 10: observed tiles, 12-hour model timeline, playback,
  fullscreen and Android Back were tested in the separate debug installation.
  A real zero-height WebView was reproduced and fixed by sizing the document from
  the measured viewport; the map itself retains flex sizing above its controls.
- Five-year export: 1,820 rows, 22 columns, 7 September 2021 through 31 August 2026.
  The August subset contained 31 rain values totalling 93.44 mm for the public Prague
  test point. Native confirmation and the Android chooser opened ChatGPT with the
  spreadsheet, question and app-language instruction. No message was sent.
- The current day and 09:00 hour were marked Today/Now at 09:18 local time.
  Vertical scrolling retained the back control; right swipe opened the next day,
  showing all 24 forecast hours.
- The store image is a real 1080×1920 capture, converted from fully opaque RGBA
  to RGB PNG without changing pixel values. The temporary display-size override
  was reset to the original 1080×2340. Temporary phone captures were removed;
  local evidence was retained.

Direct installation of the release APK returned `INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE`
because the installed Play build has a different certificate. Its data was preserved.
Physical R8/Play-binary acceptance must use the Play-delivered update; it is not
inferred from debug checks. Gemini, Claude and Grok attachment handling was not
tested on this device. The legal review's operational and accessibility boundaries
remain recorded in [the legal audit](legal-audit-2026-09-08.md).

These checks do not prove improved meteorological skill for the reported September 7
drizzle event, whose exact location and original forecast inputs remain unknown.
