Payments and refunds

Updated 8 September 2026

The current app is free

The official release does not sell the app, Premium, subscriptions or in-app items. There is no app purchase charge from this release to refund. Optional support payments take place outside the app and do not unlock paid weather features.

If a Google Play charge appears

Check the receipt, product and developer first. For any historical, mistaken or disputed Google Play purchase, use Google Play's refund help. Eligibility depends on the transaction's type, timing, location and applicable law. The EEA and UK refund guidance describes regional rules. This page does not waive a claim relating to a previous transaction.

For help identifying a Selia Weather issue, email developer Matěj Teplý at majkeylab@gmail.com with the app version and relevant order ID, date and problem description. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card details or unrelated personal information.

Optional Buy Me a Coffee support

Support is voluntary and is processed by an external platform. If a payment was duplicated, made in error or disputed, contact the creator through the support page or the email above. The platform's refund policy explains its process and escalation options. A refund is not promised automatically; mandatory rights remain unaffected.

Consumer complaints

For a problem with the app, contact support even if no payment was made. Where consumer law applies, its remedies remain available. Czech users can find complaint and out-of-court dispute information in the Czech Trade Inspection guide. Whether a dispute falls within a body's remit depends on the transaction and parties.
