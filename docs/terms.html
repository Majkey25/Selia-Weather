What the weather information means

Forecasts, calculated blends and historical grid estimates can be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Coverage and useful forecast range vary by model and location. A map forecast is sampled/interpolated model output; it is not future observed radar and does not gain extra physical resolution through interpolation. A saved coordinate does not guarantee field-level or street-level accuracy.

The app distinguishes observed radar, forecast data and NASA POWER history. History is not a record from a sensor installed at your selected point. Solar energy is not sunshine duration; daily maximum wind is not a measured gust. AI recipients can also make mistakes. Check official weather warnings for safety decisions.
