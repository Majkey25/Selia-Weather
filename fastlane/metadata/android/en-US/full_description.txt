Selia Weather brings together worldwide forecasts, radar, a custom widget and a five-year weather archive. No app account, ads or Premium purchase is required.

Ask AI about weather history. Browse daily NASA POWER estimates, choose a date range and calculate rainfall totals with data coverage. After confirmation, share the full five-year CSV with your question through a compatible installed ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Grok app. The prompt requests an answer in the weather app's language. File support depends on the recipient; a manual-upload fallback is available. The archive contains model and satellite grid estimates, not measurements from a sensor at your selected point.

The forecast uses Open-Meteo Best Match and a diagnostic multi-model calculation for up to 14 days. Nearby ČHMÚ stations in Czechia and METAR reports worldwide can supplement current conditions. Hourly forecasts remain separate from current observations. View temperature, feels-like temperature, precipitation, wind, humidity, pressure, UV and Sun/Moon details. Forecasts can be incomplete or wrong; a saved coordinate does not guarantee field-level accuracy.

Search places worldwide, save favourites, choose an exact named point on the world map, enter coordinates, or use your optional current location. The last successful forecast remains available offline.

Choose Fixed place or Follow current location. Optional following checks your device position for local weather alerts, morning briefings and widgets even when the app is closed or not in use. It requires separate disclosure and background location permission. Android may delay checks; without a fresh permitted location, alerts for the old place are paused. Select a fixed place to stop following.

The native radar map has separate Forecast and Observed modes, with zoom, pan, animation and fullscreen. ECCC GDPS provides global six-hour precipitation totals up to ten days from the model run, where data is available. DWD supplies fallback forecasts. RainViewer supplies available past radar images, with a coverage mask. The map includes a source-specific legend and keeps the last loaded frame visible while loading the next. Future maps are model output, not observed future radar. Hourly details explain precipitation probability and amount. Confidence indicators show model agreement and data limitations, not guaranteed forecast accuracy.

Build your own launcher widget. Each widget can use its own colours, transparency, gradient or custom-image background, text scale, alignment, label, and visible weather fields. If Android can no longer read a selected image, the widget uses its configured colour background and keeps working.

Selia Weather supports Android 10 and later. It follows your system language by default. You can select English, Czech, German, Spanish, or French in the app.
